Walker Building 3rd Floor Wall Panel Supplemental Map

This map was created for my Geography 467: Applied Cartographic Design class for a project centered around the new wall panels on the third floor of the Walker Building. Our maps are a supplemental material to the seven wall panels, designed to be viewed on mobile as community members explore the art. I chose to create a map to support panel five, the Meghna River in southern Bangladesh. Specifically, this map presents the seasonal monsoon migration of hilsa (Tenualosa ilisha) as a narrative journey in the Bay of Bengal and Meghna River system in Bangladesh.

Feel free to click on the map to view the full story.
